Correction: automated rumor confirmations

Earlier alpha builds displayed automated confirmation and lead-time claims that did not meet Live Wire's evidence standard.

Published July 11, 2026 · K&E Studios
Current status

The public Rumor Desk, automated publication and rumor-to-headline promotion are disabled. The prior ledger is quarantined from public output while the system is rebuilt.

What was wrong

The system could treat loose word overlap between an earlier rumor and a later article as sufficient support, without proving that the two described the same event. It could then display an automated confirmation label and claim Live Wire had identified the story first.

That logic was not reliable enough for publication. In several cases, the article used as support had been published before Live Wire's own tracked first-seen timestamp. A lead-time claim in that circumstance is invalid.

119 of 120records in the frozen pre-containment ledger carried an automated confirmation status — a rate that itself warranted immediate containment.
At least 57supporting articles in that frozen review predated Live Wire's tracked first-seen time, invalidating the associated early-call framing.

We are not repeating the underlying rumor claims here because doing so would unnecessarily republish material that has not passed verification.

What changed immediately

  • Removed automated confirmation, lead-time and “called first” presentation from public surfaces.
  • Disabled public rumor scanning, scoring, publication and promotion controls.
  • Prevented rumor records from entering the public headline or visual-preview path.
  • Quarantined the prior rumor ledger so it cannot repopulate the public interface.
  • Placed the public viewer and generated audio in a fail-closed state while containment is verified.

What must be true before the desk returns

A future rumor product will need event-level identity checks, dated source provenance, independent corroboration rules, explicit uncertainty, separation between machine suggestions and editorial decisions, and a durable audit and corrections trail. Passing automated tests is necessary; accountable editorial review is also required.

Our standard

Corrections are part of the product, not an embarrassment to hide. When Live Wire materially overstates what it knows, the right response is to stop the unsafe behavior, explain the failure plainly, preserve evidence and earn the feature back.
