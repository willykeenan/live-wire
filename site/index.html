The product is not just the show. It is the company that keeps making it.

Live Wire is the living newsroom: a KE-run operating system for turning a changing world into watchable programming while keeping sources, authority, costs, corrections, and recovery visible.

What the audience gets

  • A desk with presence — lower thirds, tickers, cast, and rhythm that read like a modern studio.
  • Characters with a point of view — fictional anchors carry the tone; the satire is intentional and labeled.
  • A network that stays accountable — source links, replayable editions, and corrections that remain part of the record.

What keeps it running

  • A governed signal desk — candidates enter with provenance instead of becoming automatic claims.
  • A production loop — accepted material becomes rundowns, characters, voice, visuals, and owned programs.
  • An operations layer — archives, corrections, budgets, recovery, and release gates keep the business healthy.

One governed loop, from signal to screen.

This is the operating model Live Wire is growing into. Each stage must carry evidence forward; no stage receives permission merely because an earlier agent produced something convincing.

A 24/7 satirical news channel with cartoon anchors.

LLM-written scripts, ElevenLabs voices, and mouth-synced cartoon anchors. The public edition ships a keyless demo. Satire is labelled. Rumors are never presented as confirmed. Operators are responsible for what they broadcast.

On air

World News

World, politics, technology, and culture as a visual desk. Public-source links remain the source of record; generated summaries and character commentary may contain errors — and when they do, the product owns the correction.

Demo

Run locally with no API keys. Invented names only. Cartoon mouth sync from synthetic timestamps until you add ANTHROPIC_API_KEY or the claude CLI, plus ELEVENLABS_API_KEY.

The cast

Eight fictional anchor concepts give the interface a point of view. Characters, voices, and commentary are generated; visual previews may be available while public audio remains gated.

Trust is the product surface.

Velocity without provenance is just content. Live Wire’s operating standard is simple: say what the system is, show where information came from, and stop when the evidence is not good enough.

⏸

Rumor desk off by default

X/Grok rumor scanning is optional and off unless you enable it. Rumors are never presented as confirmed.
